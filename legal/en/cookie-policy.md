# E-mre Control Center – Cookie Policy

**Version:** 1.0 · **Effective date:** October 12, 2026 · [Türkçe sürüm](../tr/cerez-politikasi.md)

This policy explains how **E-mre Control Center** (the "App"), a maintenance and update utility for Windows, relates to cookies and similar
tracking technologies. In short: **the App uses no cookies, stores no cookies on your computer and does not track you, so it shows no
cookie consent banner.** For other information the App processes, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 1. What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser and reads back on later visits. Cookies are used to keep you signed
in, remember preferences, count visitors or show ads. Similar technologies include advertising identifiers, tracking pixels and device
fingerprinting.

## 2. Does the App use cookies or tracking technologies?

No. E-mre Control Center is not a website but a desktop app running on your computer, and it contains no browser component that displays
web pages.

| Technology | In the App |
|---|---|
| Cookies stored on your computer | None |
| Advertising and advertising identifiers | None |
| Analytics, usage statistics, telemetry | None |
| Tracking pixels, device fingerprinting | None |
| Accounts or sign-in | None |

## 3. Cookies sent by servers during connections

To perform its functions the App connects to some services (list: [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), section 3). Some of these servers
(e.g. GitHub, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Google Forms) may send their own technical cookies in their responses. The App:

- **never writes these cookies to disk**; it keeps them only in memory, at most while the App is running, and sends them back only to the
  site that set them;
- does not create cookies itself, does not read their contents and never uses them to identify or track you or for advertising;
- discards this information completely when it closes.

Cookies are fully disabled for the speed test (Cloudflare).

## 4. Pages opened in your browser

Some links in the App open in your default browser: the legal documents and the Issues page (github.com), and the Speedtest by Ookla terms
and result page (speedtest.net). You also download the installer from GitHub's Releases page. Because these pages open in your browser,
they are subject to those sites' own cookies and policies; the App has no access to your browser's cookies.

- GitHub: [GitHub Cookies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-cookies) ·
  [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- Ookla (Speedtest): [Privacy Policy](https://www.ookla.com/privacy)

You can view and delete the cookies in your browser through the browser's own settings.

## 5. The App's files on your computer are not cookies

The App stores your settings, operation history and logs in the `%LOCALAPPDATA%\E-mre Control Center` folder. These are not cookies: no
website can read them and they are never sent to the developer. Details and how to delete them: [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md),
section 2.

## 6. Why is there no cookie consent banner?

Cookie consent is requested by services that store cookies on a device or track their users. The App does neither, so it shows no consent
banner. If this ever changes (for example, if an optional feature needs cookies), this policy and the Privacy Policy will be updated in
advance and your consent will be requested where required.

## 7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the current version is kept in the `legal/` folder of the project repository and linked from the App
(General Settings → Legal and Privacy, version 2.0.2 and later). Significant changes are noted in the release notes.
Questions: <https://github.com/E-mre-Hub/E-mre-Control-Center/issues> (please do not post personal information in a public issue).
